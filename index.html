Qualitative Field Insights (Feedback Notes)

Dominant Themes

  • Competitor pressure (Sun Pharma / Cipla) mentioned in ~12% of notes → associated with lower ΔTRx (4.2 vs 5.1)
  • Price sensitivity & generic substitution frequent in Rural / Tier-2 narratives
  • Stock-outs at Apollo / MedPlus reported in 49 notes — commercial execution risk
  • Clinical receptivity (“receptive to trial evidence”, “requested extra kits”) linked to slightly higher lift (5.3)
  • Patient adherence app and formulary inclusion requests appear as engagement signals

Sentiment Proxy

Positive clinical signals (safety, tolerability, receptivity) outnumber pure competitive/price complaints ~1.5 : 1. However, competitive intensity remains a structural headwind for share gains.

Recommendation: Pair any future high-cost conference activity with mandatory competitive messaging and formulary support packages.

Strategic Recommendations for Budget Reallocation

  • 1
    Slash / Cap Conference Sponsorship
    Conference spend (₹8.33 Cr) is the dominant ROI destroyer. Limit to top 20–30 high-potential KOLs only, or convert majority of budget into lower-cost digital + field combinations. Target: reduce conference line by 60–70% in next cycle.
  • 2
    Prioritize Chemist POB + Selective Advisory Boards
    These two channels showed the only positive incremental lift. Expand Chemist trade-margin programs in Rural & Tier-2 (higher response) and use Advisory Boards sparingly for true champions.
  • 3
    Re-engineer Digital CME & Sampling Targeting
    Current broad enrollment correlates with lower lift — likely selection or saturation. Move to propensity-based targeting (high baseline TRx + receptive language in notes) and reduce sampling volume for non-responders.
  • 4
    Territory Rebalancing
    Shift incremental budget toward Rural and Tier-2 where ΔTRx is higher. Metro currently absorbs disproportionate spend for weaker relative returns.
  • 5
    Address Stock-out & Competitive Friction
    49 stock-out complaints and frequent Sun/Cipla mentions indicate supply-chain and competitive intelligence gaps. Integrate real-time chemist stock visibility into the next campaign design.
  • 6
    Next-Cycle KPI Focus: ROAS & CPRx
    Primary KPI prioritized: Return on Ad Spend (ROAS). Secondary: Cost per Incremental Prescription. Target next cycle ROAS ≥ 0.5× (still conservative given current baseline) by eliminating low-ROI conference spend and tightening targeting.

Data Cleaning Summary (Reproducibility)

  • Deduplication: 6 duplicate HCP_IDs removed (kept first occurrence) → 494 unique HCPs
  • Dates: Mixed formats (ISO, DD/MM, MM-DD, Excel serials) standardized to YYYY-MM-DD
  • Specialties: 25 raw variants mapped → 6 standardized groups (Cardiology, Diabetology/Endocrinology, Neurology, Pulmonology, General Practice, Consulting Physician)
  • Currency: Stripped ₹/INR/commas; median imputation for missing expense lines
  • TRx: Removed “Rx”/“scripts” suffixes, filtered negatives & extreme outliers (e.g. 9999), median imputation by specialty
  • Channels: Multi-select strings one-hot encoded into 6 binary indicators
  • ROI formulae applied exactly as specified in case brief (TMI, ΔTRx, GIR, NIPL, ROMI%, ROAS, CPRx)
